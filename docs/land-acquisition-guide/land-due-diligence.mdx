# Attachment 1 — Land Due Diligence Checklist
## Ground-Up Development | Brazilian Investor Edition

> Use this checklist before submitting a final offer and during your inspection/due diligence period (typically 14–30 days after contract execution).

---

## Phase 1: Initial Feasibility (Before Making an Offer)

### 1.1 Zoning & Permitted Use

- [ ] Confirm current zoning designation (R-1, R-2, MF, etc.)
- [ ] Verify that SFR or 2–4 unit construction is **permitted by right** (no variance needed)
- [ ] Check minimum lot size requirements for the intended property type
- [ ] Check setback requirements (front, rear, side)
- [ ] Check maximum building height allowed
- [ ] Check maximum lot coverage (%)
- [ ] Verify FAR (Floor Area Ratio) if applicable
- [ ] Check if the parcel is in a **Historic District** or **Overlay Zone**
- [ ] Check for any deed restrictions or covenants (HOA, CC&Rs)

**Where to check:** County property appraiser website, city/county zoning GIS portal, or call the local Planning & Zoning department.

---

### 1.2 Utilities Availability

| Utility | Status | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| City water | Available / At street / Must extend | |
| City sewer | Available / At street / Septic required | |
| Electric | Available / Must extend | |
| Natural gas | Available / Electric only | |
| Internet/fiber | Available / None | |

- [ ] Confirm **water meter** can be obtained (some areas have moratoriums)
- [ ] Confirm **sewer connection** availability — septic adds $8K–$20K+ to cost
- [ ] Get utility availability letter from each utility provider if possible
- [ ] Check for any impact fees for new connections (can be $5K–$30K+)

**Red Flag:** If sewer is not available at street level, budget for a septic system and confirm the lot size supports it.

---

### 1.3 Lot Dimensions & Buildability

- [ ] Review survey or plat to confirm actual lot dimensions
- [ ] Calculate net buildable area after setbacks
- [ ] Confirm the buildable envelope fits your target floor plan
- [ ] Check for **easements** (utility, drainage, access) that reduce buildable area
- [ ] Check for any **encroachments** from neighboring properties
- [ ] Confirm lot shape is regular enough for your floor plan
- [ ] Verify road frontage (minimum typically 25–50 ft)

**Quick math:**
```
Buildable Width  = Lot Width − Left Setback − Right Setback
Buildable Depth  = Lot Depth − Front Setback − Rear Setback
Max Footprint    = Buildable Width × Buildable Depth × Max Lot Coverage %
```

---

### 1.4 Flood & Environmental

- [ ] Check FEMA Flood Map (floodmap.fema.gov) — identify flood zone designation
  - Zone X = outside flood plain (ideal)
  - Zone AE / VE = high-risk flood zone (avoid or budget for flood insurance + elevated foundation)
- [ ] Check if lot is in a **wetland** (National Wetlands Inventory: fws.gov/wetlands)
- [ ] Check for **environmental contamination** (EPA EnviroMapper or Phase I ESA)
- [ ] Check proximity to industrial sites, dry cleaners, gas stations (contamination risk)
- [ ] Confirm there are no **endangered species** protections affecting the parcel
- [ ] Check for **slope/grade** issues — excessive grading adds to cost

**Red Flag:** Any AE/VE flood zone designation requires additional analysis and will significantly increase insurance and construction costs.

---

### 1.5 Comparable Sales (Comps)

- [ ] Pull **land comps** — recent sales of similar vacant lots within 1 mile
- [ ] Pull **new construction comps** — recently built SFR or multifamily sold nearby
- [ ] Calculate target ARV (After Repair Value / After Build Value)
- [ ] Confirm the market supports your exit price BEFORE buying the land
- [ ] Check **days on market** for new construction — is there demand?
- [ ] Check **absorption rate** — how many new builds sell per month?

**Comp sources:** MLS (via local agent), Zillow, Redfin, county property records

---

## Phase 2: Under Contract — Due Diligence Period

### 2.1 Title Review

- [ ] Order **preliminary title report** from title company
- [ ] Review for any **liens** on the property (tax liens, mechanic's liens, HOA liens)
- [ ] Review for **easements** shown on title
- [ ] Verify chain of title is clean
- [ ] Confirm seller has legal authority to sell (if LLC/trust/estate)
- [ ] Review any **CC&Rs** or deed restrictions recorded on title
- [ ] Confirm title insurance is available (owner's policy + lender's policy)

**Action:** Have your real estate attorney review the title commitment.

---

### 2.2 Survey

- [ ] Order a **boundary survey** (if not recent survey on file)
- [ ] Confirm lot lines match legal description
- [ ] Identify all easements on the ground
- [ ] Confirm no encroachments
- [ ] Verify road frontage access

**Cost:** $500–$1,500 depending on lot size and state

---

### 2.3 Soil & Site Conditions

- [ ] Conduct **percolation test** (perc test) if septic is needed
- [ ] Consider **soil borings / geotechnical report** if:
  - There's any fill material on the lot
  - Lot is in a flood plain
  - Building will have a basement
  - Site has slope or drainage issues
- [ ] Confirm soil can support a standard slab or crawl space foundation
- [ ] Check for any **sinkholes** (important in Florida — check FLDEP sinkhole database)
- [ ] Estimate **site preparation costs**: clearing, grading, fill if needed

**Cost of geo report:** $1,500–$5,000

---

### 2.4 Permit Feasibility Confirmation

- [ ] Schedule a **pre-application meeting** with local Building Department
- [ ] Confirm the specific plan you intend to build is approvable on this lot
- [ ] Get estimated **permit timeline** (3 months? 9 months?)
- [ ] Get list of required **studies or reports** (traffic, environmental, etc.)
- [ ] Confirm **impact fees** and when they are due
- [ ] Check if any **variances or special use permits** are needed

**This step can save you from buying a lot you can't build on.**

---

### 2.5 Utilities — Final Confirmation

- [ ] Get written confirmation of water/sewer availability from utility provider
- [ ] Get estimated **tap/connection fees** in writing
- [ ] Confirm **lead time** for utility connections (some areas: 6–12 months)
- [ ] Get electric connection point confirmed with utility
- [ ] If extending utilities: get contractor estimate for extension cost

---

### 2.6 Financial Go / No-Go Analysis

Run the full **Pro Forma** (see [Deal Analysis Spreadsheet](/land-acquisition-guide/deal-analysis)) and confirm:

- [ ] Land price ≤ target acquisition cost from pro forma
- [ ] Construction estimate from GC is within pro forma budget
- [ ] Projected sale price (ARV) supported by comps
- [ ] Projected profit margin ≥ minimum threshold (recommend 20%+ of ARV)
- [ ] OR projected cash-on-cash return ≥ minimum if refinancing to hold

---

## Phase 3: Closing Checklist

- [ ] Title commitment received and reviewed
- [ ] Survey completed and reviewed
- [ ] All contingencies satisfied or waived (with informed decision)
- [ ] Proof of insurance obtained (builder's risk starts at closing)
- [ ] LLC operating agreement ready (if taking title in LLC)
- [ ] Wire instructions verified via phone (not just email — wire fraud prevention)
- [ ] Closing disclosure reviewed — confirm all fees
- [ ] ITIN provided to title company for tax reporting
- [ ] Understand FIRPTA rules do NOT apply to purchase (only to sale by foreign person)

---

## Red Flags — Walk Away If:

| Issue | Why It's a Problem |
|-------|-------------------|
| Flood zone AE/VE without clear mitigation path | Elevated foundation costs, flood insurance, financing difficulty |
| Sewer not available, lot too small for septic | Cannot build |
| Title with unresolved liens or disputes | Could lose the property or deal |
| Zoning doesn't permit intended use by right | Variance process = time + risk + no guarantee |
| Soil/fill issues without geotechnical report | Foundation failure risk |
| Utility lead times > 9 months | Delays your entire project timeline |
| New construction comps don't support your ARV | The deal math doesn't work |
| Permit timeline > 12 months | Capital tied up too long |

| Role | Purpose |
|------|---------|

| Local Real Estate Agent | Land comps, MLS access, market intel |
| General Contractor | Construction estimate before closing |
| Construction Lender | Pre-approval before you go under contract |
| Surveyor | Boundary survey |
| Civil Engineer | If site has drainage/grading complexity |
| CPA (foreign investor specialist) | FIRPTA, entity, tax planning |
