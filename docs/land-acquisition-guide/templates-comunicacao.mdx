# Templates de Comunicação em Inglês
## Modelos Prontos para Interagir com Sua Equipe Americana

> Use estes templates como ponto de partida — adapte com os dados do seu projeto. Todos foram escritos para soar profissional e direto ao ponto para o padrão americano.

---

## Dicas de Comunicação com Americanos

Antes de usar os templates, entenda o estilo de comunicação profissional americano:

| Característica | Estilo Americano | Contraste com Brasil |
|---------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Abertura** | Direto ao ponto (sem prólogo) | Mais contextualização |
| **Tom** | Informal mas profissional | Mais formal |
| **Comprimento** | Curto e objetivo | Pode ser mais longo |
| **Follow-up** | Normal e esperado | Pode parecer insistência |
| **Decisão** | Rápida, sem muita negociação inicial | Processo mais longo |
| **"Yes"** | Pode significar "talvez" — confirme por escrito | Sim geralmente é sim |

## Template 1: Primeiro Contato com Corretor Imobiliário

**Quando usar:** Ao identificar um corretor que trabalha com investidores ou terrenos na região-alvo.

```
Subject: Land Investment Inquiry – [City, State]

Hi [Agent Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I'm a Brazilian real estate investor focused on 
ground-up construction in [City]. I'm looking for infill lots or tear-down 
opportunities suitable for building a new SFR or duplex.

My target criteria:
- Budget: Up to US$[X] for land
- Zoning: R-1, R-2, or equivalent (duplex-ready preferred)
- ARV target: US$[X]–US$[X] after construction
- Timeline: Looking to close within 60–90 days

I close with an LLC and have construction financing in place. I'm a 
cash-ready buyer for the land.

Would you be available for a 15-minute call this week to discuss active 
listings and off-market opportunities?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your LLC Name]
[Phone / WhatsApp]
[Email]
```

## Template 2: Solicitação de Informações a um Credor de Construção

**Quando usar:** Ao entrar em contato com um dos credores da lista validada para entender os termos disponíveis.

```
Subject: Construction Loan Inquiry – Foreign National, Ground-Up SFR/Duplex

Hello,

I'm reaching out to learn about your construction loan programs for 
foreign national investors.

My profile:
- Nationality: Brazilian (ITIN holder, no SSN)
- Entity: [LLC Name], formed in [State]
- Project type: Ground-up construction – [SFR / Duplex]
- Location: [City, State]
- Estimated total project cost: US$[X] (land + construction)
- Projected ARV: US$[X]
- LTC requested: [X]%

I currently [have / am working to obtain] the following:
- ITIN: [Yes / Pending]
- U.S. LLC bank account: [Yes / In Progress]
- Prior real estate experience: [Brief description or "First project"]

Could you confirm whether your programs accept ITIN borrowers and share 
a rate sheet or program overview?

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[LLC Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
```

## Template 3: Solicitação de Cotação ao Empreiteiro Geral (GC)

**Quando usar:** Ao solicitar um bid formal de construção. Envie junto as plantas aprovadas ou esboço do projeto.

```
Subject: Construction Bid Request – [Address or Project Name]

Hi [GC Name],

I'm requesting a bid for a ground-up construction project at 
[address or lot description], [City, State].

Project scope:
- Type: [Single Family / Duplex / Triplex]
- Size: Approximately [X] sq ft
- Bedrooms/Bathrooms: [X BD / X BA per unit]
- Lot: [Lot dimensions or area]
- Plans: [Attached / Pending permit approval / Conceptual]

I'm looking for a turnkey bid that includes:
- Site prep, grading, and utilities connection
- Foundation, framing, roofing, windows/doors
- Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC (rough-in and finish)
- Insulation, drywall, flooring, and interior finishes
- Exterior: siding/stucco, landscaping, driveway
- All permit fees and inspections

Please exclude: land acquisition costs and financing.

I need the bid to include:
1. Itemized cost breakdown by trade/category
2. Proposed construction timeline (start to C.O.)
3. Proposed draw schedule (milestone-based, not date-based)
4. Your license number and insurance certificate

Bid deadline: [Date]

Please confirm receipt of this request and let me know if you have any 
questions.

Best,
[Your Name]
[LLC Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
```

## Template 4: Verificação de Referências do GC

**Quando usar:** Ao ligar ou enviar email para as referências que o GC forneceu.

```
Subject: Reference Check for [GC Company Name]

Hi [Reference Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I'm a real estate investor considering hiring 
[GC Company Name] for a ground-up construction project in [City].

They listed you as a reference. Would you have 5 minutes to answer a few 
quick questions?

1. What type of project did [GC] build for you? (SFR / duplex / other)
2. Was the project completed on time and on budget?
3. How was their communication throughout the project?
4. Were there any major issues? If so, how did they handle them?
5. Would you hire them again?

Any feedback is greatly appreciated. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone / Email]
```

## Template 5: Aprovação de Change Order

**Quando usar:** Ao responder a uma solicitação de change order do GC.

```
Subject: Change Order #[X] – [Project Address] – APPROVED / DENIED

Hi [GC Name],

Thank you for submitting Change Order #[X] dated [Date].

DESCRIPTION: [Brief description of change]
AMOUNT REQUESTED: US$[X]
TIMELINE IMPACT: [X] additional days

DECISION: [APPROVED / PARTIALLY APPROVED / DENIED]

[If approved:]
This change is approved as submitted. Please sign and return the 
attached Change Order form. Do not proceed until I receive the signed 
copy.

[If partially approved:]
I approve the scope of work but not the price. My counter-offer is 
US$[X] based on [reason]. Please confirm or provide justification for 
the original amount.

[If denied:]
This change is not approved at this time because [reason]. Please 
proceed with the original scope as contracted.

Please update the project schedule and budget accordingly.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Template 6: Solicitação de Draw ao Credor

**Quando usar:** Para solicitar o desembolso de um draw do empréstimo de construção.

```
Subject: Draw Request #[X] – [Project Address] – [LLC Name]

Hello,

I am requesting Draw #[X] for the project at [address].

Loan number: [X]
Draw amount requested: US$[X]
Milestone completed: [Description of completed phase]
% of construction complete: [X]%

I am attaching:
- Photos documenting milestone completion
- Conditional lien release signed by GC
- Updated draw schedule showing work completed to date

Please coordinate with your inspector for the site visit at your 
earliest convenience.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[LLC Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
```

## Template 7: Primeiro Contato com Property Manager

**Quando usar:** Ao pesquisar property managers para seu imóvel após a construção.

```
Subject: Property Management Inquiry – New Construction [SFR / Duplex]

Hi [PM Name],

My name is [Your Name]. I'm a foreign national investor (Brazilian) 
and I recently completed construction of a [SFR / duplex] at [address].

I'm looking for a property manager for:
- Property type: [SFR / 2-unit duplex]
- Estimated rent: US$[X]/month [per unit]
- Target move-in: [Month/Year]

A few questions before we schedule a call:
1. Do you have experience managing properties for international owners?
2. How do you handle fund transfers to foreign-owned LLCs?
3. What software do you use for owner reporting?
4. What is your current vacancy rate across your portfolio?
5. What are your fees (management, leasing, renewal)?

If these sound like a fit, I'd love to schedule a 20-minute call.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[LLC Name] | [Phone / WhatsApp] | [Email]
```

## Template 8: Instrução de Wire Transfer para a LLC

**Quando usar:** Para instruir advogados, credores ou corretores a enviarem recursos para a conta da LLC.

```
Subject: Wire Instructions – [LLC Name] – [Project Name/Address]

Please use the following wire transfer instructions for all funds 
related to [Project Name / Address]:

BENEFICIARY NAME:    [LLC Name]
BANK NAME:           [Bank Name — e.g., Mercury, Chase, Bank of America]
ACCOUNT NUMBER:      [XXXXXXXXXXXXXX]
ROUTING NUMBER:      [XXXXXXXXX] (ABA)
BANK ADDRESS:        [Bank Address]
MEMO/REFERENCE:      [Project Address or Transaction Reference]

Please confirm receipt and anticipated wire date.

Note: All funds must be sent to the LLC account only. Personal accounts 
cannot be used for this transaction.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[LLC Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
```

## Template 9: Notificação de Problema ao GC

**Quando usar:** Quando o inspetor ou você identifica um problema na obra que precisa ser corrigido.

```
Subject: Deficiency Notice #[X] – [Project Address] – Action Required

Hi [GC Name],

During [inspection / site visit / photo review] on [Date], the following 
deficiency was identified:

ITEM: [Description of the problem]
LOCATION: [Where in the property]
STANDARD VIOLATED: [Building code reference or contract spec, if known]

Required action:
□ Remedy the deficiency before requesting the next draw
□ Provide a written correction plan within [3 / 5] business days
□ Coordinate with inspector for re-inspection after correction

This is a formal written notice per our contract agreement. Please 
confirm receipt and your plan of action.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[LLC Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
```

## Template 10: Encerramento de Contrato com Prestador de Serviço

**Quando usar:** Para encerrar contrato com GC, PM ou qualquer prestador de forma profissional e documentada.

```
Subject: Notice of Contract Termination – [Project/Property Address]

[GC Name / PM Name],

This letter serves as formal notice of termination of our agreement 
dated [Contract Date] for [property address / project], effective 
[Date] (30 days from today) per Section [X] of our agreement.

[If with GC — add:]
As of the termination date:
- All work in progress should be completed to a safe and secure state
- All materials purchased for the project and stored on-site remain 
  property of [LLC Name]
- A final accounting of draws paid vs. work completed will be requested
- All warranties and lien releases remain in effect

[If with PM — add:]
Please transfer the following within 14 days of termination:
- All tenant files (leases, screening reports, payment history)
- All security deposits (held in escrow)
- Remaining operating reserve balance
- Keys, access codes, and vendor contacts

Please confirm receipt of this notice.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[LLC Name] | [Phone] | [Email]
```

## Vocabulário Essencial: Inglês Imobiliário para Construtores

### Termos de Construção

| Inglês | Português | Uso Comum |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Lot / Parcel** | Terreno / Lote | "We're purchasing a 6,000 sq ft lot" |
| **Grading** | Terraplanagem | "Grading and site prep: US$8,000" |
| **Utilities hookup** | Ligação de utilidades | "Water/sewer hookup: US$12,000" |
| **Framing** | Estrutura/Esqueleto | "Framing inspection passed" |
| **Rough-in** | Instalações brutas | "Rough-in electrical and plumbing done" |
| **Drywall** | Gesso acartonado | "Drywall installation starts Monday" |
| **Certificate of Occupancy (C.O.)** | Habite-se | "C.O. issued on [date]" |
| **Draw** | Desembolso | "Ready to request Draw #3" |
| **Punch list** | Lista de pendências | "We have a 12-item punch list" |
| **Change order** | Ordem de mudança | "GC submitted a change order for US$4,500" |
| **Retainage** | Retenção | "10% retainage held until C.O." |
| **Lien** | Gravame / Ônus | "Make sure there are no liens on title" |
| **Lien release** | Liberação de gravame | "Get lien release before each draw" |
| **Subcontractor (sub)** | Subempreiteiro | "The electrical sub starts tomorrow" |
| **Bid** | Proposta / Cotação | "I'm collecting 3 bids for the project" |

### Termos Financeiros e Imobiliários

| Inglês | Português | Uso Comum |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **ARV** | Valor pós-reforma/construção | "ARV is estimated at US$420,000" |
| **LTC** | Empréstimo sobre custo total | "Lender offers 80% LTC" |
| **LTV** | Empréstimo sobre valor | "DSCR loan at 75% LTV" |
| **DSCR** | Índice cobertura serviço dívida | "DSCR is 1.28 — we qualify" |
| **Closing costs** | Custos de fechamento | "Budget 2%–3% for closing costs" |
| **Escrow** | Conta escrow (custódia) | "Draw funds are held in escrow" |
| **Title insurance** | Seguro de título | "Title insurance is required by lender" |
| **HOA** | Associação de moradores | "No HOA in this neighborhood" |
| **Property tax** | IPTU | "Annual property tax: US$4,200" |
| **Appraisal** | Avaliação de imóvel | "Appraisal came in at US$415,000" |
| **Cap rate** | Taxa de capitalização | "Duplex has a 6.2% cap rate" |
| **Cash-on-cash return** | Retorno sobre caixa investido | "8.5% cash-on-cash after refi" |

### Expressões Úteis em Negociação

| Situação | Expressão em Inglês |
|----------|---------------------|
| Fazer oferta | "I'd like to submit an offer at US$___" |
| Pedir desconto | "Is there flexibility on the asking price?" |
| Confirmar termos | "Can we confirm this in writing?" |
| Pedir extensão de prazo | "I need a 7-day extension on the due diligence period" |
| Recusar (diplomaticamente) | "We've decided to pass on this opportunity at this time" |
| Solicitar urgência | "Is there any way to expedite the [inspection / approval]?" |
| Pedir referências | "Could you provide 2–3 references from similar projects?" |
| Confirmar pagamento | "Wire sent — please confirm receipt" |

## Comunicação por WhatsApp / Texto com o GC

Americanos usam SMS/texto para comunicações rápidas. Para documentação formal, sempre use email. Para atualizações rápidas:

```
ATUALIZAÇÃO DIÁRIA (sugestão de texto para pedir ao GC):
"Hi [GC], can you send me a quick photo update today? Thanks"

CONFIRMAÇÃO DE MARCOS:
"Hi [GC], foundation inspection passed — great! Please confirm
you're on track for framing start next Monday."

SOLICITAÇÃO DE RELATÓRIO:
"Hi [GC], can you send the weekly update report by Friday EOD?
Need photos + % complete + any open issues. Thanks."
```

> **Importante:** WhatsApp e SMS são para comunicação rápida. **Decisões financeiras, change orders, aprovações e reclamações sempre por email** — cria registro legal.
